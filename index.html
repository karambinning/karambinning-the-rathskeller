No. 01 — Welcome

Set inside a Grade-listed merchant building a stone's throw from Hanse House, The Rathskeller pours a handpicked wine list alongside a seasonally rotating bistro menu built around Norfolk produce. Up at the bar: signature cocktails, real ales, a long whisky shelf. Down in the cellar: live music most weekends.

The room is small. The candles do most of the lighting. The kitchen rewrites the menu when the produce moves on. Bookings are gratefully taken — walk-ins, as the room allows.

No. 02

The Bistro

A short, seasonal kitchen menu — Norfolk produce, suppliers within a short drive of the quay, no shortcuts. The menu rewrites itself as the season turns.

Wed — Sun · Lunch & dinner

No. 03

The Wine Bar

A handpicked wine list, real ales, lagers, bottled beer, a long shelf of spirits and a small but ferocious cocktail menu. The bar staff have opinions; ask.

From noon · Wine flights on request

No. 04

The Cellar

Down the stairs is a vaulted music room — local acts, the occasional touring name, late drinks. Private hire welcome for parties, anniversaries, wakes done with warmth.
