What
we serve.

A short kitchen menu, rewritten as the season turns.
A wine list, handpicked.
A bar that takes cocktails seriously.
A cellar room for live music & private hire.

No. 01 — The Bistro Kitchen

From the kitchen.

  • A short à la carte Small plates, mains and puddings built around Norfolk produce. The card rewrites itself as suppliers change. By the season
  • Sunday lunch Roasts and trimmings, served from noon while the room holds out. Booking strongly encouraged. Sundays only
  • Afternoon & pre-theatre Lighter plates between services — a glass of something, a bite, a quiet hour by the window. Walk-ins as the room allows
No. 02 — The Wine Bar

At the bar.

  • Handpicked wine list By the glass, the carafe, the bottle. Old-world spine, new-world surprises. Flights on request when the room is quiet. Glass · Carafe · Bottle
  • Real ales, lagers & bottled beer A small rotation of cask alongside the regulars. Ask what's on this week. Ask the bar
  • Whisky, rum & the long spirits shelf A deep shelf of single malts, rums and small-producer spirits, talked about by people who like talking about them. By the measure
  • Cocktails — signature & classic A short list the bar team rewrites when they find something better. Tell us a flavour and we'll build to it. On the card
No. 03 — The Cellar

Down the stairs.

  • Live music nights Local acts most weekends, with the occasional touring name. Bar runs late. See Instagram
  • Private hire — parties & events A vaulted brick-arched room for birthdays, anniversaries, work parties and wakes. Held warmly. On enquiry
  • Tasting events & supper clubs Wine and spirit-led tasting nights, occasional supper clubs. Announced via the Instagram first. Limited covers
No. 04 — Notes on the house

Things to know.

  • Dietary needs Tell us when you book. The kitchen will build something to fit; please call ahead for severe allergens. Always
  • Children & dogs Children are welcome at the table; dogs are welcome at your feet, water bowl provided. Welcome
  • Accessibility Step-free to the bistro and bar. The cellar is down a flight of stairs — call ahead if we can plan around it. Call ahead

The menu rewrites itself with the season. For tonight's card or to ask a question of the kitchen, the phone is the fastest answer.
