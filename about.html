A Cellar by the Quay

Independently run since 2019. A small kitchen, a long bar, a music room beneath the floor.

The Rathskeller sits at No. 1 South Quay, on the Lynn waterfront where Hanseatic merchants once unloaded wine, cloth and timber. The name borrows from the German Ratskeller — the council cellar beneath a town hall, traditionally a place to eat and drink. The building keeps that brief.

The room upstairs is a wine bar and bistro, lit largely by candles, with seating tight enough that conversations carry and reservations are kindly requested. The kitchen rewrites the menu against whatever Norfolk produce the suppliers are turning out — game in the autumn, vegetables in summer, fish off the boats year round.

Down a flight of stairs is the cellar — a vaulted, brick-arched room that the previous tenants used for private dining and the current operators returned to its older purpose: a music room. Local acts most weeks, late drinks, the occasional touring name. It hires for parties, birthdays, anniversaries, and, with the right warmth, wakes.

The current operators took over in late 2019 — a family-led ownership trading as Passion Taverns Ltd — and rebuilt the kitchen around a refined bistro brief: short menu, named suppliers, accessible prices. The wine list is handpicked rather than corporate; the cocktail menu changes when the bar staff find something better.

Children are welcome at the table. Dogs are welcome at your feet. The room is step-free at the entrance — the cellar music room is down a flight of stairs, so if you'd like to plan around that, do call.

For tonight's bookings, music line-up or kitchen times, the phone is the fastest answer — 01553 773 713.
